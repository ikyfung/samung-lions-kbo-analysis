# Samsung Lions KBO Analysis

## Project overview

This is a data analytics portfolio project that investigates the factors associated with wins and losses for the Samsung Lions during the 2025 KBO regular season.

The project will analyse team performance by game, including home-versus-away performance, opponent matchups, runs scored and allowed, starting pitching, and bullpen performance.

## Business question

What factors are most strongly associated with Samsung Lions wins and losses during the 2025 KBO regular season?

## Stakeholder

Samsung Lions coaching and performance-analysis team.

## Project scope

- **Team:** Samsung Lions
- **League:** Korea Baseball Organization (KBO)
- **Season:** 2026 regular season
- **Unit of analysis:** One row represents one Samsung Lions game
- **Excluded:** Postseason games, cancelled games, and predictive machine-learning modelling in the first version

## Analytical questions

1. How do win rate, runs scored, and runs allowed differ between home and away games?
2. Which opponents have the lowest Samsung Lions win rate?
3. Are losses against specific opponents mainly associated with low scoring or high runs allowed?
4. How do runs scored, runs allowed, and run differential differ between wins and losses?
5. Is starting pitcher workload, measured by innings pitched, associated with team win rate?
6. Are late-inning runs allowed associated with a higher likelihood of losing?
